## Where to start

Three editorial picks: a register that prices every channel by the second with the source page on each row, a review site that scores tools against published criteria, and the arena where models are voted on blind. An editor's choice, not a ranking; everything below is alphabetical.

1. [RenderDrama](https://renderdrama.com/) — What a second of AI video costs, channel by channel. Data: CSV, CC BY-SA 4.0.
2. [SinCoSphere](https://sincosphere.com/) — Criteria-based reviews and comparisons of AI short-drama and video generation tools, each score tied to a published criterion.
3. [Artificial Analysis — Text to Video leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/text-to-video) — Blind pairwise human votes on text-to-video models, published as an Elo leaderboard; an image-to-video board sits beside it.
